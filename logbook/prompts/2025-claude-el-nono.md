**STEP 2: Conduct Thorough Research**

**You use multiple sources:**

**Research Hierarchy:**
1. **Hammock's Own Knowledge Base**
   - What have we already learned?
   - What patterns have we observed?
   - What's documented in our notes?

2. **Verified External Sources**
   - Academic research
   - Industry reports
   - Case studies from credible sources
   - Expert analysis

3. **Community Knowledge**
   - Reddit discussions
   - Expert forums
   - Professional communities
   - Real practitioner experiences

4. **Tool Documentation**
   - Official docs
   - Best practices
   - Known limitations

**Research Quality Standards:**
- ✅ Multiple sources (never rely on just one)
- ✅ Recent information (check dates)
- ✅ Credible sources (evaluate authority)
- ✅ Practical evidence (real examples, not just theory)
- ✅ Conflicting viewpoints (acknowledge debate)
